# Four-Twelve — English narration

The 55-second top-of-funnel cut, for LinkedIn and paid social. Script by Fable,
retimed and trimmed; the two-minute film owns the explanation, this one lands one
feeling and one promise.

Runtime ~57s. 13 lines. Read at roughly 2.6 words per second — the budgets below
are what the picture is cut to, so a line that runs long pushes its scene.

Figures are the English capture's own and must not be invented: $412.68 in tolls
across 39 crossings in one week, $106.82 charged back to the renter.

**The echo is the point.** "Four-twelve in the afternoon" in the first line and
"four hundred twelve dollars" in the last scene are the same number coming back as
a bill. Do not change one without the other.
