# Ad copy — the film on Instagram

The English film for `instagram.com/myeztoll`. Instagram shows two lines before "more"
and makes no link in a caption clickable, so the hook is short, the address is written
out, and the argument sits underneath for whoever opens it.

Video: `social/linkedin-en-720p.mp4` (7.5 MB — under the 10 MB the browser tools will
carry) or the 1080p master `myeztoll-toonbee-en.mp4` when the file is picked by hand.
A landscape 16:9 film posts fine; Instagram frames it as a reel.

---

## Caption — English

A toll is billed to a plate, and the plate belongs to you.

That is why rental fleets absorb charges they never incurred. The agency knows which vehicle went through the gantry; it has no idea who was driving. By the time the invoice clears, the rental is closed, the deposit is back with the renter, and the only name left on the paperwork is yours.

MyEZToll closes that gap. We read from the toll networks themselves — thirty-one agency systems covering practically all of the United States — match every crossing against the booking that was open at that minute, and charge the card the renter already left on file. Camera violations go the same way.

It costs the fleet owner nothing: no subscription, no set-up fee, nothing per vehicle. You are paid the toll in full, plus a share of the fee we charge the renter. The one thing we sell is our own telematics hardware, and it is optional.

A minute and a half, and you can see the whole loop — myeztoll.com

## Caption — Español (el corte en español, su propio post)

El peaje se le cobra a la placa, y la placa es suya.

Por eso las flotas de renta terminan absorbiendo cargos que nunca generaron. La agencia sabe qué vehículo cruzó; no tiene idea de quién iba manejando. Para cuando llega la factura, la renta está cerrada, el depósito ya volvió a la cuenta del cliente, y el único nombre que queda en el papel es el suyo.

MyEZToll cierra esa brecha. Leemos directamente de las redes de peaje —treinta y un sistemas de agencias que cubren prácticamente todo Estados Unidos—, cruzamos cada paso con la reserva que estaba abierta en ese minuto y cobramos a la tarjeta que el cliente ya dejó registrada. Con las multas de cámara pasa lo mismo.

Al dueño de la flota no le cuesta nada: sin suscripción, sin costo de instalación, sin cargo por vehículo. Usted recibe el peaje completo, más una parte de la comisión que le cobramos al cliente. Lo único que vendemos es nuestro propio equipo de telemática, y es opcional.

Minuto y medio, y ve el circuito completo — myeztoll.com

#rentadeautos #flotas #peajes #ezpass #sunpass #turohost #telematica #negocios #autosdealquiler #rentacar

## Notes

- Same claims as `linkedin-posts.md` and `facebook-post-film.md`; the checking table
  there covers this copy too.
- **The English cut went up 2026-09-07:** https://www.instagram.com/myeztoll/reel/Dc_R7hCNSP1/
  — 16:9 so the landscape frame survives, AI label on, caption added afterwards through
  More options -> Edit (the post had gone out bare).
- **One language per post.** The Spanish cut went up the same day as its own reel:
  https://www.instagram.com/myeztoll/reel/Dc_Th9dNEW0/ — Instagram will not let media be
  added to a post that is already published, and a caption carrying both languages reads
  worse than either. The Spanish is US/Latin-American and aimed at the **same** audience
  as the English one: rental fleets in the United States, the Spanish-speaking half of
  them (renta, carro, manejar — never peninsular).
- The first Share attempt on the Spanish reel died on the spinner without an error and
  nothing appeared on the profile; the same steps a second time went through. Watch the
  dialog to "Your reel has been shared." before believing it.
- **The clipboard is shared with the app.** A caption pasted from it can turn out to be
  whatever was copied last — the Spanish caption came out as a stray line of chat text on
  the first paste. Re-set the clipboard immediately before pasting and read the character
  counter before sharing.
- The profile was fixed the same day: picture from
  `aegis-aa-web/images/ez_logo_linkedin_1000.jpg`, name **Aegis AA Soft -> Aegis AO Soft**
  (Accounts Center -> Profiles -> myeztoll -> Name; Instagram allows only two name changes
  in fourteen days).
- Meta Business Suite *can* publish to the linked Instagram account without that sign-in,
  but its composer keeps no `input[type=file]` in the DOM: "Add photo/video" opens the
  operating system's file picker, which the browser tools cannot drive. On
  facebook.com the inputs are there, which is why the Facebook reel went up unattended.
- The display name reads **Aegis AA Soft** and has to become **Aegis AO Soft** — AO Soft
  is the company that develops the product.
