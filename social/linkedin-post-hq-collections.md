## Post — English

Two new parts of our owner-portal series are up: seven episodes, English and Spanish, free to watch.

**If you run your fleet in HQ Rental Software**, the portal can read it directly — your cars, your bookings, your customers. Four episodes cover the whole connection: the two keys HQ gives you and where to find them, what comes across on its own, the two things HQ does not send (the state a plate belongs to, and the toll tag — without which no toll can ever reach the car), and where a toll ends up: back on the reservation in HQ as an external charge, receipt attached, taken from the card HQ already holds.

**The other three are about money that never arrives.** When the card fails and the reminder series runs out, a debt can be referred to a collections agency. The episodes follow it the way an owner meets it: which debts are worth referring at all — and which are not, what to read on a case before deciding, and what comes back when the agency collects the debt in full, or only half of it.

Nothing in these videos is a mock-up. Every screen is the live portal, with personal data blurred.

Watch on the site: myeztoll.com/tutorials — en español: myeztoll.com/es/tutoriales
Or on the channel: youtube.com/@myeztoll
