# Ad copy for the film — Aegis AO Soft LLC

Written as two posts, one per recording. **Only the English one was published**
(2026-09-05); the Spanish copy below is kept for the other channels the Spanish cut is
aimed at — the site, email, a paid campaign — and is not queued for LinkedIn.

the post explains the mechanism the story leaves implicit — why a rental fleet absorbs
charges it never incurred, and what closes that gap. Someone who reads the post and then
watches learns two different things, which is the only reason to run both.

## Post 1 — English

A toll is billed to a plate, and the plate belongs to you.

That single fact is why rental fleets absorb charges they never incurred. The agency knows which vehicle went through the gantry; it has no idea who was behind the wheel. By the time the invoice clears, the rental is closed, the deposit is back in the renter's account, and the only name left on the paperwork is yours.

MyEZToll closes that gap. We read from the toll networks themselves — thirty-one agency systems covering practically all of the United States — match every crossing against the booking that was open at that minute, and charge the card the renter already left on file. Camera violations go the same way.

For the fleet owner it costs nothing. No subscription, no set-up fee, nothing per vehicle, and connecting a GPS you already run is free too. You are paid the toll in full, plus a share of the fee we charge the renter — so a recovered charge is revenue, not a wash.

The one thing we sell is our own telematics hardware, and it is optional.

A minute and a half, and you can see the whole loop:
https://myeztoll.com

## Post 2 — Español (not published on LinkedIn)

El peaje se le cobra a la placa, y la placa es suya.

Ese solo hecho explica por qué las flotas de renta terminan absorbiendo cargos que nunca generaron. La agencia sabe qué vehículo cruzó; no tiene idea de quién iba manejando. Para cuando llega la factura, la renta está cerrada, el depósito ya volvió a la cuenta del cliente, y el único nombre que queda en el papel es el suyo.

MyEZToll cierra esa brecha. Leemos directamente de las redes de peaje —treinta y un sistemas de agencias que cubren prácticamente todo Estados Unidos—, cruzamos cada paso con la reserva que estaba abierta en ese minuto y cobramos a la tarjeta que el cliente ya dejó registrada. Con las multas de cámara pasa lo mismo.

Al dueño de la flota no le cuesta nada. Sin suscripción, sin costo de instalación, sin cargo por vehículo, y conectar el GPS que ya usa tampoco cuesta. Usted recibe el peaje completo, más una parte de la comisión que le cobramos al cliente: lo recuperado es ingreso, no un empate.

Lo único que vendemos es nuestro propio equipo de telemática, y es opcional.

Un minuto y medio, y ve el circuito completo:
